Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Firewood Company ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with this website.

How You Can Contact Us

This website does not have a contact form. The phone and email links on this site simply open your own phone or email app so you can reach us directly. That conversation happens between you and The Firewood Company — not through this website — and this website does not collect, store, or process any personal information itself.

Information We Receive

If you call or email us, we naturally receive whatever information you choose to share with us, such as your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, and details about your order. We use this information only to respond to you and fulfill your order. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties.

Cookies and Analytics

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. If that changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those practices.

Third-Party Services

This website relies on a few third-party services to operate:

  • Google Fonts — used to display the typefaces on this site. Loading these fonts may share your browser's IP address with Google, subject to Google's own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages — used to host this website, which may log standard technical information (like IP addresses) for security purposes.
  • Google Maps — our Contact section links out to Google Maps for directions to our location. Once you click that link, you're subject to Google's privacy policy, not ours.

We don't control these third parties' privacy practices and encourage you to review their policies if you have questions.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Privacy Rights

Since this website doesn't collect personal information on its own, there's nothing stored here to access, correct, or delete. If you'd like us to delete information you've shared with us directly (for example, in a past email), just let us know and we'll take care of it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website or business practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
