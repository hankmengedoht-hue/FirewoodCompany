White Oak vs. Hickory: Which Should You Choose?

A side-by-side look at our two most popular hardwoods.

← Return to Firewood Guide

White oak is the better choice for everyday home heating, burning dense and long with low smoke and steady heat. Hickory is the better choice for cooking and BBQ, with a bold aroma and intense heat that's prized by smokers, pizza ovens, and pit masters. Both are excellent hardwoods; the right one depends on what you're using it for.

White Oak

Dense and long-burning, with low smoke and steady heat. Excellent for fireplaces and wood-burning stoves, and a reliable choice if you're heating your home through the winter.

Hickory

Bold aroma and intense heat. The go-to for BBQ pits, smokers, and cooking fires, and a favorite with the restaurants and commercial kitchens we supply.

Our Recommendation

If you're heating a home, start with white oak or oak. If you're cooking or smoking, hickory is worth the choice.
